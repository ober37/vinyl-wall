PART A — covers and details
1. `list` the "records" collection. Pick documents (skip `deleted: true`) where `needsEnrich` is true OR `coverAsset` is missing/empty OR `runtime` is missing/empty.
2. For each, look it up by artist + title with the shell (python urllib) against these JSON APIs, in order, until a confident match:
   - Deezer: https://api.deezer.com/search/album?q=<artist title>, then https://api.deezer.com/album/<id> for label, release_date and duration (seconds). Cover: the search result's cover_xl.
   - iTunes: https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=<artist title>&entity=album&limit=15 (artworkUrl100 with 100x100bb replaced by 600x600bb); runtime = sum of trackTimeMillis from https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<collectionId>&entity=song.
   - MusicBrainz (User-Agent "VinylWall/1.0"): https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/release-group/?query=releasegroup:<title> AND artist:<artist>&fmt=json, cover via https://coverartarchive.org/release-group/<id>/front-500.
   Covers come first from Discogs when the record is linked there: if the doc has `coverHint`, use that image; otherwise, if it has a `discogsId`, GET https://api.discogs.com/releases/<discogsId> (User-Agent "VinylWall/1.0", no token) and use the uri of the image with type "primary" (else the first image). That is the exact pressing, so it beats the APIs above; still use them for runtime, and for the cover when Discogs has no image.
   Confident = album title matches (ignore case, punctuation, a leading "The", " - Single", "(Deluxe…)") and artist matches allowing small typos, a missing/extra "s", or "The". Prefer full albums over singles/EPs.
   For runtime, use the STANDARD edition: ignore singles/EPs and anything with fewer than 6 tracks, and avoid deluxe/remaster/box editions with bonus tracks when a standard one exists. If the record's pressing field names a special edition with extra tracks, that edition's length is fine. Sanity check against what you know of the album; if the API result is clearly a box set or a single, use the standard album length you know instead.
3. Set autoFilled to the union of the old list and what you filled. Always set needsEnrich and coverHint to {"__delete__": true}. If no confident match for the cover: append to notes {"d": "<today YYYY-MM-DD>", "t": "Auto-lookup couldn't find a confident match for the cover. PART F — About: artist and album info (after part A)
Use only these public sources, with the header User-Agent "VinylWall/1.0 (https://github.com/ober37/vinyl-wall)" and at least 1 second between MusicBrainz requests. Never write a link that didn't come from a typed Wikidata property, MusicBrainz or Wikipedia as described here, and never one from your own knowledge.
F1. `list` the "records" collection. Take at most 50 per run; the rest wait for the next run.
F2. Find the album's MusicBrainz release group by artist + title (https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/release-group/?query=releasegroup:<title> AND artist:<artist>&fmt=json). Accept only a confident match by part A's rules. From the match take the release group id and the id of its first artist credit.
F3. Album: GET https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/release-group/<id>?inc=url-rels&fmt=json and find the relation of type "wikidata" (…/wiki/Q…). GET https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Special:EntityData/<Q>.json. Build `about` from what you find (leave out anything you have no value for):
   - summary: take the English Wikipedia article (sitelinks.enwiki.title) intro as plain text with GET https://en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php?action=query&prop=extracts&exintro=1&explaintext=1&redirects=1&format=json&titles=<title, URL-encoded>. Write the summary yourself from that text only, adding no facts it doesn't state: a lede of one or two sentences (at most about 40 words) on what the album is, then a blank line ("\n\n"), then a second paragraph of two to four sentences on its context and why it matters, about 120 words in all. Plain text, no markdown. Skip it if there's no article, it's a disambiguation page, or it isn't about this album.
   - wiki: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/<title with spaces as underscores>; source: "claude"; at: "<today YYYY-MM-DD>".
   - released: the earliest P577 (publication date) as "YYYY-MM-DD" when the day is known, else "YYYY".
   - label: the English label of the first P264 (record label). producers: English labels of up to three P162 (producer). genres: English labels of up to three P136 (genre). Get labels for those Q ids with GET https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php?action=wbgetentities&ids=<Q1|Q2|…>&props=labels&languages=en&format=json.
   - recorded: the English labels of P483 (recorded at), joined with ", ", then " · " and the recording dates if the Wikipedia intro states them (e.g. "Columbia 30th Street Studio, New York · March 2 and April 22, 1959"). Leave it out if neither is known.
   - tracks: when the record has a `discogsId`, GET https://api.discogs.com/releases/<discogsId> (no token) and use its tracklist entries with type_ "track": [{"pos": position, "title": title, "dur": duration as "m:ss"}], with tracksSource "discogs" and tracksUrl https://www.discogs.com/release/<discogsId>. Otherwise GET https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/release?release-group=<id>&inc=recordings&status=official&fmt=json&limit=25, prefer a release whose media format is vinyl (else the earliest), and use its media tracks: pos = the track "number" (vinyl releases number them A1, B2 …; otherwise use the plain number), title, dur = length in ms as "m:ss" (leave dur out when unknown); tracksSource "musicbrainz", tracksUrl https://musicbrainz.org/release/<release id>.
F4. Artist: if the "artists" document with that MusicBrainz artist id exists, its fetchedAt is less than 180 days old and its source is "claude", reuse it. Otherwise GET https://musicbrainz.org/ws/2/artist/<id>?inc=url-rels&fmt=json, follow its "wikidata" relation to the Wikidata entity, and build:
   - summary and wiki the way F3 does for the album, about the artist: a lede of one or two sentences, a blank line, then two to four sentences on their career and why they matter, written only from the English Wikipedia intro; source "claude";
   - links, one per kind, from the first non-deprecated value of each property, and only these: P856 official website → website (use the URL itself, https only); P2003 Instagram username → https://www.instagram.com/<v>/ (instagram); P2397 YouTube channel ID → https://www.youtube.com/channel/<v> (youtube); P1902 Spotify artist ID → https://open.spotify.com/artist/<v> (spotify); P3283 Bandcamp profile ID → https://<v>.bandcamp.com/ (bandcamp); P2850 Apple Music artist ID → https://music.apple.com/us/artist/<v> (applemusic); P3040 SoundCloud ID → https://soundcloud.com/<v> (soundcloud); P7085 TikTok username → https://www.tiktok.com/@<v> (tiktok); P2002 X username → https://x.com/<v> (x); P2013 Facebook username → https://www.facebook.com/<v> (facebook). Percent-encode the value, and drop any value with "/", "?", "#" or spaces in it.
   If there is no English Wikipedia page for the artist and the record has a `discogsId`, fall back to Discogs: GET https://api.discogs.com/releases/<discogsId> (no token), take artists[0].id, GET https://api.discogs.com/artists/<that id>, and use its `profile` with Discogs markup removed ([a=Name] → Name, [l=Label] → Label, drop [url=…] tags and other [..] codes) as the summary, cut to three sentences, with "source": "discogs" and "wiki": https://www.discogs.com/artist/<that id>. Discogs `urls` are free text: never use them as links.
   `set` "artists"/<MusicBrainz artist id> to {"name", "wikidata": "<Q…>", "summary", "wiki", "source", "links": [{"kind","url"}], "fetchedAt": now in milliseconds}. Leave out fields you have no value for.
F5. `update` the record (if_version = its version) with only: artistRef = the MusicBrainz artist id (when an artist was found), about (when F3 found anything), and aboutAt = "<today YYYY-MM-DD>". Then `update` settings/site (if_version = its version) with discogs fields: on 200 → status "ok", verifiedUser = the returned username, msg "", checkedAt = now as "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC"; if the returned username differs from discogs.username (case-insensitive), still set status "ok" but msg "Token belongs to <returned username>; sync will use that account." and use the returned username for part C.
